Built on local knowledge and proper process.

We work with municipalities, developers and communities across KZN to deliver road and civil infrastructure that meets contract specification from the outset not just on completion.

Every project runs through the same process: site establishment, procurement of materials and subcontractors, construction and quality control, then documented hand-over. That consistency is what lets us take on both small access-road jobs and larger municipal contracts.

As a registered private company, MDG also manages procurement and supplier compliance for the contracts we run so clients get one point of accountability for both the build and the paperwork behind it.
